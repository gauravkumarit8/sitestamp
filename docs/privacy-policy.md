# SiteStamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

SiteStamp ("the app") is a camera app that stamps the date, time, GPS location
and a note onto photos. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

**Developer / contact:** grvthakur, gaurav954624@gmail.com

## Short version

- No account is needed.
- Your photos, notes and settings stay on your device.
- The app has no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell your data.
- The optional address lookup uses your phone's own location service.

## What the app accesses

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take photos. |
| Location (while the app is open) | To read your GPS position and print it on the photo. The app does not use background location. |
| Photo library (save only) | To save finished photos to a "SiteStamp" album on your device. |

## What is stored, and where

- **Photos:** saved to your device's gallery. They include the stamp and GPS
  coordinates in the image metadata. They are not uploaded by the app.
- **Notes and settings:** your recent notes and the address on/off setting are
  stored locally on your device.
- **Temporary files:** working copies made while saving a photo are deleted
  right after the photo is saved.

## Address lookup

If "Show address on photo" is on, the app asks your phone's built-in geocoding
service to turn your coordinates into a street address. On most Android phones
this service is provided by Google, so your coordinates may be sent to that
provider under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not send your
location to our servers. You can switch this off in the app's settings.

## Purchases

SiteStamp may offer an optional "Pro" upgrade as a subscription or a one-time
purchase. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your
card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro and keeps a
local note on your device so Pro works offline for a limited time.

## Sharing

We do not receive, sell or share your photos or location. If you share a photo
from your gallery, the stamp and location data in it go wherever you send it.

## Children

SiteStamp is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Your choices

- Turn off the address lookup in the app's settings.
- Revoke camera or location permission at any time in your phone's settings.
- Delete photos from your gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes
  its notes and settings.

## Changes

If the app changes how it handles data (for example by adding accounts, cloud
backup or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
