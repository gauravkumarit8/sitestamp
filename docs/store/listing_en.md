# Play Store listing (English)

## App name (max 30)
SiteStamp: GPS Photo Proof Cam

## Short description (max 80)
Job photo camera that stamps GPS location, date, time and notes on every photo.

## Full description (max 4000)
SiteStamp is a simple camera for people who need to show where and when a photo was taken. Every picture is stamped with the date and time, GPS coordinates, altitude, GPS accuracy, the street address (optional) and a note you type, so the proof is part of the image itself.

WHO IT IS FOR
- Site inspectors, contractors and technicians
- Delivery, field sales and service teams
- Surveyors, property and rental inspectors
- Anyone who needs a date, time and location stamp on a photo

WHAT YOU GET
- Date, time and time zone printed on the photo
- Latitude, longitude, altitude and GPS accuracy
- Street address from your location (can be turned off)
- Your own note on each photo, such as "Before", "After" or "Site visit"
- Quick note chips: your recent notes are one tap away
- Live GPS status: see "Acquiring GPS" or "GPS Ready" before you shoot
- Warning if a mock (fake) location is detected
- Front and back camera, flash control
- Photos are saved to a "SiteStamp" album in your gallery
- GPS coordinates are also written into the photo's metadata
- Clean, simple screen with no clutter

DESIGNED TO BE RELIABLE
- GPS starts as soon as the camera opens, so a fix is usually ready when you tap the shutter
- Photos are saved upright so they look right in your gallery
- Images are resized for fast saving and low memory use

PRIVACY
SiteStamp has no account and no ads. Your photos and notes stay on your device. The optional address lookup uses your phone's location service. See the privacy policy for details.

PLEASE NOTE
GPS accuracy depends on your device and surroundings and can be weaker indoors. A stamped photo is a helpful record, but it is not a tamper-proof or legally certified document.

Permissions used: Camera (to take photos) and Location while the app is open (to stamp where the photo was taken).
