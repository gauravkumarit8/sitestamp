# Photo checklists

A checklist is the list of photos a job needs. Pick one in the camera screen (the
Checklist chip). The next unfinished step is suggested; tap any step to shoot it
instead. Each photo is stamped with its step ("Step 4/8: Inverter and its label")
and the step is ticked off. Progress is kept per project and can be restarted.

## Built-in examples
Rooftop solar installation, Civil works progress, Maintenance visit, Delivery proof.
These are STARTING POINTS. Different schemes, clients and departments ask for
different photos. Always check the exact photo requirements of your scheme or client
and make a custom checklist that matches (New checklist: name + one step per line).

## Reports
CSV has a `checklist_step` column; PDF captions and share messages include the step.
